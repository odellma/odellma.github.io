# Current projects

## Fisheries Genome visualization tool

I am interested in developing computational tools that integrate genomic sequence data, gene annotations, and functional pathway information to investigate patterns of genome organization relevant to conservation genomics. Using publicly available resources such as NCBI, Ensembl, Gene Ontology Consortium, KEGG, and UniProt, I plan to build a cloud-based genome visualization platform that explores how genes involved in related physiological systems and biochemical pathways are distributed across chromosomes. Through this work, I hope to better understand genome architecture and identify approaches for discovering informative genetic markers that can support studies of population structure, adaptation, and species conservation.
